About these enrollment measures

Annual enrollment is the average of 12 month-end Medicaid enrollment counts, not the number of unique people enrolled during the year. Years with incomplete monthly data are excluded from annual comparisons. Preliminary records are retained with their data-quality flags. The state filter applies to the enrollment cards and monthly trend; the map and rankings compare all eligible states.

About quality coverage and comparability

Maps and rankings use eligible rates for the selected reporting year. Trends use a fixed set of states meeting the primary comparability criteria across 2023–2025. A measure with no primary cohort will show an explanation instead of a trend. These cohorts are not nationally representative. Reporting year may differ from the measurement period.
